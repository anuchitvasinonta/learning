# Week 1 Phrase Sheet — Foundations

Characters are shown for reference only (menus/signs will use them) — the
actual goal is pinyin + audio, not character reading.

## How to hear real native audio for each line

No direct clickable links here on purpose — I can't verify an audio URL
actually resolves from this environment, so rather than hand you dead
links, every row below is tagged with which tool to use. The **Characters**
column is the exact string to paste into that tool's search box:

- **Pleco** — single words and short fixed expressions (most of Days 1-4,
  plus a few short items later). Paste into the main search bar, tap the
  speaker icon on the matching entry.
- **Youglish** (youglish.com, set language to Chinese) — full
  sentences/questions, where natural rhythm and context matter more than
  a word-by-word dictionary clip (most of Day 5, and the sentence-level
  items in Day 6). Paste the string, drop trailing `?`/`…`, hit search —
  it jumps to real YouTube clips of native speakers saying it in context.

## Tones primer (do this before anything else)

Mandarin syllables carry a tone that changes meaning. Same syllable `ma`:

| Tone | Mark | Example | Meaning | Contour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | ā | mā (妈) | mother | flat, high |
| 2nd | á | má (麻) | hemp | rising |
| 3rd | ǎ | mǎ (马) | horse | dips low, then rises |
| 4th | à | mà (骂) | scold | falls sharply |
| neutral | a | ma (吗) | question particle | short, toneless |

Use Yoyo Chinese's free tone videos on YouTube for this — watching a
native speaker's pitch contour helps more than any written explanation.
Don't try to memorize tone rules yet; just get your ear used to hearing
the difference.

## Day 2 — Courtesy & basic responses

| Characters | Pinyin | English | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 请 | Qǐng | Please | Pleco |
| 不好意思 | Bù hǎoyìsi | Excuse me / sorry (casual, most common) | Pleco |
| 对不起 | Duìbuqǐ | Sorry (for an actual mistake, stronger) | Pleco |
| 没关系 | Méi guānxi | It's okay / no problem (reply to sorry) | Pleco |
| 是 | Shì | Yes / is | Pleco |
| 不是 | Bú shì | No / is not | Pleco |
| 对 | Duì | Right / correct | Pleco |

Note: in casual tourist situations, 不好意思 (bù hǎoyìsi) does double duty
as both "excuse me" (getting attention) and "sorry" — it's the one to
default to.

Week 2 when ordering food.

## Day 4 — Numbers 11-100 (the pattern, not new words)

Mandarin numbers are fully regular past 10 — no new vocabulary, just
combination:

| Characters | Pinyin | English | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 十一 | shíyī | 11 (10+1) | Pleco |
| 十二 | shí'èr | 12 | Pleco |
| 二十 | èrshí | 20 (2×10) | Pleco |
| 二十一 | èrshíyī | 21 | Pleco |
| 三十 | sānshí | 30 | Pleco |
| 五十 | wǔshí | 50 | Pleco |
| 九十九 | jiǔshíjiǔ | 99 | Pleco |
| 一百 | yībǎi | 100 | Pleco |

Pattern: `[tens digit] + 十 + [ones digit]`. Drill this by having a
native-audio source (ChinesePod has a numbers lesson) read random numbers
and saying them back before you see the answer.

## Day 5 — "I don't understand" / clarification

These are the single most useful phrases for the whole trip — you'll use
them constantly in the first week there. All full sentences, so use
Youglish for natural rhythm rather than Pleco's word-by-word clips.

| Characters | Pinyin | English | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 我听不懂 | Wǒ tīng bu dǒng | I don't understand (what you said) | Youglish |
| 请再说一次 | Qǐng zài shuō yī cì | Please say it again | Youglish |
| 请说慢一点 | Qǐng shuō màn yīdiǎn | Please speak a bit slower | Youglish |
| 你会说英语吗 | Nǐ huì shuō Yīngyǔ ma | Do you speak English? | Youglish |

## Day 6 — Asking price / wanting things

| Characters | Pinyin | English | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 多少钱 | Duōshao qián? | How much money? (how much does it cost) | Youglish |
| 这个多少钱 | Zhège duōshao qián? | How much is this? | Youglish |
| 这个 | Zhège | This (one) | Pleco |
| 那个 | Nàge | That (one) | Pleco |
| 我要 | Wǒ yào... | I want... / I'd like... | Youglish |
| 我不要 | Wǒ bù yào | I don't want (it) | Youglish |
| 块 | kuài | ...yuan (colloquial, like "bucks") — used when stating a price | Pleco |

## Day 7 — Review & integration

Recycle everything by shadowing this mini dialogue end to end, then
record yourself doing both parts. For audio on the full lines, search
each sentence in Youglish; the individual words were already covered
Days 1-6.

> A: 你好! (Nǐ hǎo!) — Hello!
> B: 你好! (Nǐ hǎo!) — Hello!
> A: 这个多少钱? (Zhège duōshao qián?) — How much is this?
> B: 二十块。(Èrshí kuài.) — 20 yuan.
> A: 谢谢!再见! (Xièxiè! Zàijiàn!) — Thanks! Bye!

If you can do this from memory, fluently, at natural speed — without
reading — Week 1 is done. That's the bar; move to Week 2 (food) regardless
of whether every single phrase above feels perfect, since Week 2-4 will
keep recycling greetings/numbers/survival phrases anyway.
